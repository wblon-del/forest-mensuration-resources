# Forest Mensuration — Week-by-Week Teaching Plan

## Suggested 14-week course

| Week | Topic | Lecture focus | Practical/assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction | Scope, applications, instruments, accuracy and precision | Instrument familiarization |
| 2 | Diameter | DBH, circumference, special stems, errors | Practical 1 |
| 3 | Height | Total/merchantable height, clinometer and laser methods | Practical 2 |
| 4 | Basal area | Individual and stand basal area, density | Numerical tutorial |
| 5 | Volume | Volume concepts, form factor, equations | Practical 4 |
| 6 | Form and taper | Stem form, taper, sectional methods | Taper exercise |
| 7 | Growth | Diameter/height/volume increment, PAI, MAI, CAI | Growth calculations |
| 8 | Midterm | Review and examination | Midterm examination |
| 9 | Sampling | Population, sample units, fixed-area and systematic sampling | Sampling design |
| 10 | Inventory | Plot establishment, field protocols, quality control | Practical 3 |
| 11 | Biomass | Allometry, wood density, above-ground biomass | Biomass exercise |
| 12 | Carbon | Carbon estimation, assumptions and uncertainty | Carbon exercise |
| 13 | Data analysis | Cleaning, summaries, graphs, interpretation | Dataset exercise |
| 14 | Integration | Inventory case study and revision | Final review |

## Recommended teaching sequence

Use the pattern **concept → worked example → field demonstration → student practice → interpretation**. Emphasize units and measurement consistency throughout the course.

## Suggested assessment weighting

- Practical/field work: 20%
- Exercises/tutorials: 10%
- Midterm: 20%
- Project/inventory assignment: 15%
- Final examination: 35%

The weighting can be adjusted to institutional requirements.
