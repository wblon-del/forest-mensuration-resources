# Practical 1: Measurement of Tree Diameter (DBH)

## Objective
To measure diameter at breast height (DBH) accurately and consistently and calculate basal area.

## Equipment
Diameter tape or calipers, measuring tape, tree tags/numbering system, field notebook or mobile data sheet, pencil, and calculator.

## Procedure
1. Identify the adopted breast-height reference point (commonly 1.3 m above ground).
2. Measure the reference height along the stem.
3. Inspect the stem for buttresses, forks, swelling, vines or other abnormalities.
4. Place the diameter tape perpendicular to the stem axis at the selected measurement point.
5. Read DBH to the required precision.
6. Record species, tree number, DBH and any measurement remarks.
7. Repeat a subset of measurements for quality control.

## Calculation
For DBH in cm:

`BA = π × DBH² / 40,000`

## Questions
1. What is the adopted DBH reference height?
2. What should be done when the stem is irregular at the reference height?
3. Calculate basal area for three measured trees.
4. Compare repeated measurements by two observers.

## Quality-control note
Never silently change an unusual value. Check the tree and original field record before deciding whether it is a measurement error or a genuine observation.
