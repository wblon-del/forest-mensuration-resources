# Practical 4: Tree Volume and Sectional Measurement

## Objective
To understand stem-volume estimation and compare simple form-factor and sectional approaches.

## Equipment
Diameter tape/calipers, measuring tape, log scale, calculator, field sheet and appropriate safety equipment.

## Procedure
1. Select an approved sample stem or log. Do not fell a tree solely for this exercise unless authorized.
2. Mark measurement sections at defined intervals.
3. Measure section diameters consistently.
4. Record section lengths and diameters.
5. Calculate sectional volumes using the method prescribed by the instructor.
6. Compare the result with a form-factor estimate where appropriate.

## Common sectional formulas

**Huber's formula:**
`V = g_m × L`

where `g_m` is cross-sectional area at the middle of the section and `L` is section length.

**Smalian's formula:**
`V = [(g_1 + g_2)/2] × L`

where `g_1` and `g_2` are end cross-sectional areas.

**Newton's formula:**
`V = [g_1 + 4g_m + g_2]/6 × L`
