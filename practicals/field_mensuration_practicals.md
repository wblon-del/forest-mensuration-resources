# Forest Mensuration Field Practicals

## Practical 1: DBH and circumference

### Objective
Measure tree diameter consistently and accurately.

### Equipment
Diameter tape or measuring tape, DBH staff, tree tags or chalk, field sheet and pencil/device.

### Procedure
1. Establish the standard measurement point.
2. Identify the stem condition and apply the approved protocol for irregular trees.
3. Measure circumference or diameter.
4. Record tree number, species, measurement height, value and remarks.
5. Repeat selected measurements for quality control.

### Output
A verified table of at least 20 tree measurements.

## Practical 2: Tree height

### Objective
Estimate total tree height using an appropriate hypsometer or clinometer method.

### Procedure
1. Select a tree with a clearly visible base and top.
2. Measure or establish the required horizontal distance.
3. Sight the tree top and base according to the instrument method.
4. Calculate or read the height.
5. Repeat selected trees and compare results.

### Output
Height measurements for at least 10 trees and a short discussion of measurement error.

## Practical 3: Basal area and stand density

### Objective
Calculate tree basal area, plot basal area, trees/ha and basal area/ha.

### Output
A plot summary table with calculations and units.

## Practical 4: Tree and stand volume

### Objective
Estimate tree and plot volume using an approved volume equation, volume table or form-factor approach.

### Output
Tree-level volume, total plot volume and volume/ha.

## Practical 5: Forest inventory sampling

### Objective
Design and implement a small fixed-area sample inventory.

### Output
Sampling map or layout, field data, stand summary and discussion of sampling limitations.

## Safety and ethics

Follow field safety procedures, work in teams where appropriate, avoid damaging vegetation unnecessarily, and comply with all applicable forest and protected-area rules and institutional field protocols.
