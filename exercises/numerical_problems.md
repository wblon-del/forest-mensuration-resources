# Forest Mensuration Numerical Exercises

## Instructions

Show formulas, substitutions, units and final answers. Use π = 3.1416 unless otherwise stated.

### 1. Diameter

A tree has circumference 157.08 cm. Calculate DBH.

### 2. Basal area

Calculate the basal area of a tree with DBH 50 cm.

### 3. Trees per hectare

A 0.04-ha plot contains 32 trees. Calculate trees/ha.

### 4. Basal area per hectare

A 0.05-ha plot contains trees with a combined basal area of 2.75 m². Calculate basal area/ha.

### 5. Tree volume

A tree has DBH 40 cm, height 18 m and applicable form factor 0.46. Calculate estimated stem volume.

### 6. Stand volume

A 0.1-ha plot has an estimated total tree volume of 16 m³. Calculate volume/ha.

### 7. Periodic annual increment

Stand volume increases from 210 to 258 m³/ha over 6 years. Calculate PAI.

### 8. Mean annual increment

A stand has produced 360 m³/ha by age 36 years. Calculate MAI.

### 9. Sampling expansion

A 0.05-ha plot has 4.2 m² of basal area. Estimate basal area per hectare.

### 10. Carbon

An approved biomass equation gives 420 kg above-ground biomass for a tree. Using a carbon fraction of 0.47, estimate carbon mass.
