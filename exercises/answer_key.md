# Forest Mensuration Exercise Answer Key

1. D = 157.08 / 3.1416 = **50.0 cm**.

2. g = π(0.50²)/4 = **0.19635 m²**.

3. Trees/ha = 32 / 0.04 = **800 trees/ha**.

4. BA/ha = 2.75 / 0.05 = **55 m²/ha**.

5. g = π(0.40²)/4 = 0.12566 m². V = 0.12566 × 18 × 0.46 = **1.039 m³** approximately.

6. V/ha = 16 / 0.1 = **160 m³/ha**.

7. PAI = (258 − 210) / 6 = **8 m³/ha/year**.

8. MAI = 360 / 36 = **10 m³/ha/year**.

9. BA/ha = 4.2 / 0.05 = **84 m²/ha**.

10. Carbon = 420 × 0.47 = **197.4 kg C**.

## Teaching note

Accept equivalent answers where rounding is reasonable. Require students to show units and the formula used. For volume and biomass questions, emphasize that the equation or form factor must be appropriate to the stated application.
