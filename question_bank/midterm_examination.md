# Forest Mensuration — Midterm Examination

**Suggested duration:** 2 hours  
**Total:** 50 marks

## Section A — Multiple Choice (10 × 1 = 10 marks)

Select the best answer. Use MCQ Sets 1 and 2 as a question pool and avoid repeating questions already used in class.

## Section B — Short-answer questions (5 × 2 = 10 marks)

1. Define forest mensuration and state four applications.
2. Explain accuracy and precision with one field example each.
3. Describe the correct procedure for measuring DBH.
4. Explain basal area and why it is useful in stand description.
5. Identify three major sources of tree-height measurement error.

## Section C — Numerical problems (3 × 5 = 15 marks)

1. A tree has DBH 45 cm. Calculate its basal area.
2. A 0.05-ha plot contains 32 trees and 2.20 m² total basal area. Calculate trees/ha and basal area/ha.
3. A tree has DBH 50 cm, height 20 m and form factor 0.50. Estimate volume using `V = g × H × f`.

## Section D — Applied question (15 marks)

Design a field exercise to estimate stand density and basal area in a community forest. State the sampling unit, plot size, measurements to collect, boundary rule, field equipment, data-quality checks and calculations required.

## Marking guidance

Award marks for correct concepts, formulas, logical field procedures, appropriate units and clear interpretation. For numerical questions, award partial credit for correct formula and substitution even if an arithmetic error occurs.
