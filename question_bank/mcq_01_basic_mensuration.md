# Forest Mensuration MCQs — Set 1

Choose the best answer.

1. DBH most commonly refers to:
   - A. Diameter at branch height
   - B. Diameter at breast height
   - C. Diameter below height
   - D. Diameter of basal height

2. If circumference is 62.83 cm, diameter is approximately:
   - A. 10 cm
   - B. 15 cm
   - C. 20 cm
   - D. 25 cm

3. Basal area of a tree is calculated from:
   - A. Height only
   - B. Crown width only
   - C. Stem diameter at the defined reference point
   - D. Tree age only

4. One hectare contains:
   - A. 100 m²
   - B. 1,000 m²
   - C. 10,000 m²
   - D. 100,000 m²

5. A 0.05-ha plot contains 20 trees. Trees/ha equals:
   - A. 100
   - B. 200
   - C. 400
   - D. 500

6. The quadratic mean diameter gives relatively greater mathematical weight to:
   - A. Smaller trees
   - B. Larger trees
   - C. Missing trees
   - D. Seedlings only

7. Stem taper describes:
   - A. Increase in crown size
   - B. Decrease in stem diameter toward the top
   - C. Increase in tree age
   - D. Change in soil depth

8. MAI is calculated as:
   - A. Age / yield
   - B. Yield × age
   - C. Yield / age
   - D. Yield + age

9. A fixed-area plot is useful because:
   - A. Its area is known
   - B. It contains every tree in the forest
   - C. It eliminates all sampling error
   - D. It requires no boundary rule

10. A major source of non-sampling error is:
   - A. Measuring only a sample
   - B. Incorrect DBH measurement
   - C. Natural variation in forests
   - D. Population size

## Answer key

1-B, 2-C, 3-C, 4-C, 5-D, 6-B, 7-B, 8-C, 9-A, 10-B
