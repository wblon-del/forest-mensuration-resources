# Forest Mensuration MCQs — Set 2

1. Stand basal area is commonly expressed as:
   - A. cm/ha
   - B. m²/ha
   - C. m³/ha
   - D. kg/ha

2. The quadratic mean diameter is calculated using:
   - A. √(ΣD²/n)
   - B. ΣD/n²
   - C. ΣD²/n²
   - D. √(ΣD/n)

3. Stem taper generally refers to:
   - A. Reduction in diameter toward the top
   - B. Increase in height with age
   - C. Reduction in tree age
   - D. Increase in basal area only

4. Form factor relates actual tree volume to:
   - A. Crown volume
   - B. A reference cylinder
   - C. Root biomass only
   - D. Plot area

5. A 0.1-ha plot with 45 trees represents:
   - A. 45 trees/ha
   - B. 90 trees/ha
   - C. 450 trees/ha
   - D. 4,500 trees/ha

6. PAI over a 10-year interval is:
   - A. Total yield divided by age only
   - B. Change in the attribute divided by 10 years
   - C. Age divided by change
   - D. Change multiplied by 10

7. In a fixed-area plot, the expansion factor is:
   - A. Plot area
   - B. 1/plot area in hectares
   - C. Plot perimeter
   - D. Tree count × plot area

8. A major purpose of forest sampling is to:
   - A. Measure every tree
   - B. Estimate population attributes from a representative sample
   - C. Eliminate all uncertainty
   - D. Avoid recording field data

9. Which is a non-sampling error?
   - A. Variation caused by observing only a sample
   - B. Incorrect DBH recording
   - C. Random selection of plots
   - D. Natural diameter variation

10. Biomass equations should preferably be:
   - A. Chosen without regard to species or site
   - B. Validated and appropriate for the population being estimated
   - C. Applied with any units
   - D. Used outside their calibration range without qualification

## Answer key

1-B, 2-A, 3-A, 4-B, 5-C, 6-B, 7-B, 8-B, 9-B, 10-B
