# Module 7: Growth and Yield

## 7.1 Growth

Growth is the increase in a tree or stand variable over time. Variables may include diameter, height, basal area, volume or biomass.

## 7.2 Periodic annual increment

For a variable X measured at two times:

**PAI = (X₂ − X₁) / number of years**.

Example: stand volume increases from 180 to 220 m³/ha over 5 years.

PAI = (220 − 180) / 5 = **8 m³/ha/year**.

## 7.3 Mean annual increment

For cumulative production X at age A:

**MAI = X / A**.

MAI is especially useful in yield analysis when cumulative production and stand age are known.

## 7.4 Growth measurement

Repeated measurements can be used to calculate increment. Permanent sample plots are especially valuable for monitoring change over time.

## 7.5 Yield

Yield describes the amount of forest production available or accumulated under defined conditions, such as volume per hectare at a particular age.

## Exercise

A stand contains 300 m³/ha at age 30 years. Calculate MAI.

**Answer:** 300 / 30 = **10 m³/ha/year**.
