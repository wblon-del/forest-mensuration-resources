# Forest Mensuration — Quick Formula Reference

| Topic | Formula | Typical unit |
|---|---|---|
| Diameter from circumference | `D = C/π` | cm or m |
| Basal area | `BA = πD²/4` | m² |
| BA from DBH in cm | `BA = πD²/40,000` | m² |
| Trees per hectare | `N/ha = n/A` | trees/ha |
| BA per hectare | `BA/ha = ΣBA/A` | m²/ha |
| Arithmetic mean DBH | `D̄ = ΣD/n` | cm |
| Quadratic mean diameter | `Dq = √(ΣD²/n)` | cm |
| Form factor | `f = V/(gH)` | dimensionless |
| Tree volume | `V = gHf` | m³ |
| Huber sectional volume | `V = g_mL` | m³ |
| Smalian sectional volume | `V = [(g₁+g₂)/2]L` | m³ |
| Newton sectional volume | `V = [(g₁+4g_m+g₂)/6]L` | m³ |
| Periodic annual increment | `PAI = (V₂−V₁)/(t₂−t₁)` | attribute/year |
| Mean annual increment | `MAI = V/A` | attribute/year |
| Biomass per hectare | `B/ha = B_plot/A` | t/ha |
| Carbon | `C = B × carbon fraction` | t C |
| Sampling intensity | `SI = sampled area/population area × 100` | % |
| Fixed-plot expansion factor | `EF = 1/A` | per ha |

## Unit reminders

- 1 ha = 10,000 m²
- 1 m = 100 cm
- Convert DBH to metres before using `πD²/4` when the result is required in m².
- Always report the unit with the final answer.

## Important caution
The formulas are teaching references. For operational forestry work, use the applicable institutional protocol, approved volume equations and validated biomass/allometric models.
