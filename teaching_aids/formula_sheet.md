# Forest Mensuration Formula Sheet

## Diameter and circumference

D = C / π

C = πD

## Basal area

For D in metres:

g = πD² / 4

For DBH in centimetres:

g = πD² / 40,000

## Plot expansion

Variable per hectare = variable measured in plot / plot area in hectares

Expansion factor = 1 / plot area (ha)

## Trees per hectare

Trees/ha = trees counted / plot area (ha)

## Simple tree volume

V = g × H × f

where f is the applicable form factor.

## Smalian sectional volume

V = L(A₁ + A₂) / 2

## Periodic annual increment

PAI = (X₂ − X₁) / number of years

## Mean annual increment

MAI = X / age

## Simple carbon estimate

Carbon = Biomass × carbon fraction

## Reminder

Always state units, reference height, measurement method, equation source and applicable range when reporting mensuration results.
