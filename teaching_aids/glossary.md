# Forest Mensuration Glossary

**Accuracy** — Closeness of a measurement to an accepted or true value.

**Basal area** — Cross-sectional area of a tree stem at a defined reference diameter; stand basal area is commonly expressed in m²/ha.

**CAI (Current Annual Increment)** — Growth during a specified annual period.

**DBH** — Diameter at breast height, measured at the adopted reference height.

**Diameter distribution** — Number of trees represented in defined diameter classes.

**Form factor** — Ratio of actual tree volume to the volume of a reference cylinder.

**Growth** — Change in a tree or stand attribute over time.

**MAI (Mean Annual Increment)** — Cumulative yield divided by stand age.

**Merchantable height** — Stem height meeting specified product or minimum-diameter requirements.

**PAI (Periodic Annual Increment)** — Average annual increase in a measured variable over a specified period.

**Precision** — Degree to which repeated measurements agree with each other.

**Sampling intensity** — Proportion of the population area represented by the sample.

**Stand density** — Number of trees per unit area, commonly trees/ha.

**Taper** — Decrease in stem diameter from the base toward the top.

**Tree volume** — Quantified stem or merchantable material, commonly expressed in m³.

**Yield** — Quantity of forest production available or accumulated under specified conditions.
