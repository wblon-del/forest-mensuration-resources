# Measurement Diagrams — Teaching Briefs

These briefs can be converted into classroom diagrams or slides.

## Diagram 1 — DBH measurement

Show a standing tree, ground line and a horizontal measurement line at the adopted breast-height reference. Label the reference height, diameter tape and stem axis. Add a second panel showing an irregular stem and the institutionally approved alternative measurement point.

## Diagram 2 — Tree-height measurement

Show the observer, horizontal distance to the tree, line of sight to the base, line of sight to the top, top angle and base angle. Add the tangent relationship used by the selected method.

## Diagram 3 — Basal area

Show a circular stem cross-section and shade the cross-sectional area enclosed by the stem circumference. Label radius and diameter and show `BA = πD²/4`.

## Diagram 4 — Stem taper

Show a tree stem with diameter measurements at several heights. Draw a simple diameter-versus-height profile to demonstrate taper.

## Diagram 5 — Fixed-area plot

Show a square or circular plot within a forest stand. Label plot boundary, plot area, eligible trees and boundary-tree decision rule.

## Diagram 6 — Sampling grid

Show a forest polygon with regularly spaced sample points and a clearly marked random starting point. Explain that the actual design must follow the approved inventory protocol.

## Diagram 7 — Sectional volume

Show a stem divided into sections with diameters at the ends and middle. Label `g1`, `gm`, `g2` and section length `L`, then present Huber, Smalian and Newton formulas.

## Diagram 8 — Growth

Show a simple time series of diameter or volume against age and identify periodic growth, MAI and CAI conceptually.

## Visual design guidance
Use large labels, simple line drawings, consistent units and minimal text. Each diagram should communicate one measurement concept and include a short caption describing the field implication.
